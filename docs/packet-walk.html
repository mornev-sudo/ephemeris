Where a packet can leave the pipeline

StageCounterWhy

Counters are the names ephctl status shows under drops. "Handed over" entries aren't losses: the packet went to the control plane (DHCP, PPPoE) or to the router's own SSH and portal. With a packet category at debug (ephctl log set route debug), every drop is also logged with its reason and addresses.
